How to control your boat

Hold → release in the gold window → recover → hold again at the next gold window. The gold window moves. Press W for waves. Hold A/D or a side button to pull with both sculls, one side stronger. Right stronger steers left; left stronger steers right. Release to end the correction. Space pulls evenly. Buoy contact costs speed.

Game privacy

Your nickname is saved in this browser for your next visit. You can change it in the name field or remove it by clearing browser data. Practice races remain in your browser. Ranked race inputs are sent to Supabase for verification. Nickname, race time and creation time are public. Cloudflare Turnstile performs an invisible bot check when you start a ranked race. For rate limits, the server stores a daily-changing hash of your IP address; provider logs may contain additional access data. Race credentials and rate-limit entries older than one day are cleaned up at the next valid race start. Do not include personal contact information in your nickname.
