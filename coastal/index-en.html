How to play

On the beach: use ↑ / ↓ to run forward and backward, and ← / → to move sideways. Press W to jump over driftwood. On mobile, hold the direction buttons. Driftwood is solid and can only be crossed by jumping. There is no timing bar while running.

In the boat: hold and release in the gold window, then recover. A / D or the side buttons pull both sculls, one side stronger: Left stronger turns the boat right; Right stronger turns it left. Press W to handle a wave.

Three buoys stand in a straight line. On the way out, pass them left, right, then left. Golden gates mark the correct side. After the 180° turn, row straight back to the beach. Buoys are solid: each collision slows you and adds a 3-second penalty. Cross-waves hit the full water area and push the boat off course; W reduces their effect. In a duel, you race a replay of the best verified run.

Privacy and ranked races

Practice times and your player name stay in your browser. For ranked races, the server verifies the full race. Name, time and the best run’s control inputs are public for the leaderboard and ghost. An invisible Cloudflare check protects the race start. Rate limits and processing follow the information in SCULL / 200. Turnstile Privacy Addendum.
